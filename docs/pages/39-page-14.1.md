# 14. SURVEYS AND PROBES

## 14.1 SURVEYS

A survey (SUR) will report the number of natural resource deposits on a planet, their deposit location numbers, the resource each deposit contains, quantity of resource per deposit, and the planet's habitability number.
A survey also reports the number of TL-1 farms assembled and the total population of the open colonies there.
A SUR can only be made if the planet and s/c are in the same orbit.
A SUR is ordered with a survey (SUR) order, is completed in one turn, and requires one TPT and one PRO to do it.

## 14.2 PROBES

There are three types of probes that may be made.
All three are ordered with a probe (PRB) order.

### 14.2.1 ORBIT PROBES

An orbit probe lists the s/c's at the planet,the log base 10 of the total mass of each kind of resource in the unmined deposits on the planet, the log base 10 of each s/c's mass, its ID#, its name, the type of planet and its habitability number.
An orbit probe cannot be made of the orbit the s/c is in because s/c's automatically get a probe report of the orbit they are in every turn.
An orbit probe can be made of any other orbit in the system; in a multiple star system, all orbits in the systems can be probed from one orbit.
The orbit probe also lists the tactical coordinate positions of the s/c's at the planet.

### 14.2.2 S/C PROBES

A s/c probe lists the log base 10 the mass of a s/c, its population, its assembled STUN, LTSU, or SLSU, and its structural requirements.
Both the s/c doing the probing and the s/c being probed must be in the same orbit.

### 14.2.3 SYSTEM PROBES

A system probe will give the planet types by orbit; and a list of coordinates and distances of all the stars within a number of light years, depending on the magnitude of the probe.
Each magnitude equals one light year.
A magnitude 3 probe can probe 3 light years.
