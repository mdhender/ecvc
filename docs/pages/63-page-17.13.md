### 17.4.41 DISBAND ORDERS (DSB)

- Format: S/C ID#, "DSB", quantity, units to be disbanded.
- Example: 66, DSB, 7E3, TRN-1.
- Notes: Only SLD or TRN can be disbanded.
  (SPYS and CNW cadres must be dis-assembled).

### 17.4.42 PAY ORDERS (PAY)

- Format: S/C ID#, "PAY", amount per unit, POP type.
- Example: 38, PAY, .09, USK.
- Notes: Pay orders are standing orders only used for USK, PRO, and SLD.
  Race numbers are not shown in pay orders.

### 17.4.43 RATION ORDERS (RTN)

- Format: S/C ID#, "RTN", % of food.
- Example: 16, RTN, 95.
- Notes: The RTN order rations the population of colony 16 to 95% of the standard amount of food.
  The percent sign is not used in the order.
  RTN orders are standing orders.

### 17.4.44 CONTROL ORDERS (CON)

- Format: Colony #, "CON".
- Example: 775, CON.
- Notes: Colony 775 wishes to control the world on which it is located.

### 17.4.45 UNCONTROL ORDERS (UNC)

- Format: Colony #, "UNC".
- Example: 681, UNC.
- Notes: Colony 681 releases control of the world on which it is located.

### 17.4.45 NAME ORDERS (NAM)

- Format: S/C ID#, "NAM", s/c name.
- Example: 39, NAM, Dragon Fire.
- Notes: The NAM order gives the name of Dragon Fire to s/c 39.

### 17.4.46 PLAYER NAME ORDERS (NOM)

- Format: Player ID#, "NOM", player name.
- Example: 15, NOM, Bad Bob.
- Notes: The NOM order gives the name of Bad Bob to player 15.

### 17.4.47 PLANET NAME ORDERS (NAP)

- Format: S/C ID#, "NAP", planet name.
- Example: 88, NAP, Goldball.
- Notes: The NAP order has s/c 88 name the planet it is on: Goldball.

### 17.4.48 NEWS RELEASE ORDERS (NSR)

- Format: S/C ID#, "NSR", text, SIGNED (or UNSIGNED).
- Example: 150, NSR, Space drives for sale next turn. SIGNED.
- Example: 33, NSR, Bad Bob is a traitor. UNSIGNED.
- Notes: If the NSR is signed, the printout will show the player's correct ID#, name, and s/c #.
  If the NSR is not signed, the word "UNSIGNED" will be on the printout.
  News releases must be in the context of the game, must not be obscene, or personally attack another player.
  They may also include a player's real name, address, and phone number.
