#### 15.6.6.2 Commitment Percentage

When s/c's with multiple-TL EWP's and MSS's are involved in bombardment, the percentage commitment (percent of weapons committed) applies equally to each MSL and EWP the s/c has, regardless of the TL of each.

#### 15.6.6.3 Weighted Average

#### 15.6.6.5 More Miscellaneous

ESH's, MSL's, and EWP's (used defensively) never use more than enough fuel (and in the case of MSL's, anti-missiles), to stop an attack.
For instance, just enough ANM are used to stop incoming MSS's even if the s/c has more MSL's and ANM available.
If the target s/c has multiple-TL ESH's, the highest TL ESH will be used first.
Ships always take 10 times the mass units in damage that a colony does.
For instance, a colony hit with one MSS-1 loses 100 M.U.: a ship hit with one MSS-1 loses 1,000 M.U. (Ships are more compact than colonies).
A ship may not fice an EWP or MSL unless it has at least one assembled sensor on board.
A colony can't fire on a ship unless it has an assembled SEN.
A colony can fire on another colony without a SEN.
Damage from bombardment against untargeted colonies and against ships is allocated randomly.
STUN, LTSU,and SLSU all absorb damage at the same rate.
(500 M.U. damaged will destroy 1,000 STUN or 1,000 LTSU or 1,000 SLSU).
On ships, the number destoyed will be 10,000 instead.
Ship maneuvers during combat take place on a 3-D grid, with the planet at 0/0/0.
The scale is 1 distance unit = 10,000 km's.
Troops and weapons are not as likely to be hit as other items are, even if they are in the designated target group.
Ships do not colide.
