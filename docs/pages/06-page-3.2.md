### 3.3.1 TYPES OF PLANETS, cont.

of planets: gas giants, terrestrial, and asteroids.

#### 3.3.1.1 Gas Giants

Gas Giants have hugh atmospheres.
Surface colonies are only located on the moons of these planets.
Gas giants and their moons are treated as one body.

#### 3.3.1.2 Terrestrial

Terrestrial planets are too small to retain the hugh atmospheres of gas giants, but large enough to have become spherical.
Terrestrial in this context does not necessarily mean earthlike or habitable.

#### 3.3.1.3 Asteroids

An asteroid is so small that it is able to retain its irregular shape.
The term "asteroid" in this game will refer to an entire asteroid belt, and is also considered to be a planet.
The asteroids in an orbit are treated as one body.

### 3.3.2 HABITABILITY NUMBERS

A habitability number indicates the number of population units who can live on a planet's surface, in OPEN colonies, without being overcrowded or having to import food.
Habitability numbers do not affect, and are not affected by, the operations of enclosed surface colonies.
Each planet has a habitability number ranging from zero to twenty-five.
Should a planet have a population exceeding the limit indicated by the habitability number, the death rate of *all* the OPEN colonies on the planet will be increased.
(Further explained in section 5.3).

### 3.3.3 RESOURCE DEPOSITS.

Each planet has a maximum of 40 natural resource deposits.
Each deposit contains 1,000,000-99,000,000 units of: gold, fuel, metallics and non-metallics.

## 3.4 TIME SCALE

One turn in the game equals one quarter of a (galactic standard) year.

## 3.5 INITIAL PRINTOUT AND TURNSHEET

When a player enters a game, he receives an initial printout which lists: the number of factory groups he has, the number of factories per group, the groups' technological level, and the kind of unit each group makes.
It also lists the type of population units he has, and their quantity; the types of weapon units he has and their quantity; the types of transportation he has and their quantity; the types of resources he has and their quantity, and any misc. items he has.
The printout also shows his player ID#, his home colony's ID#, his race number, and a free magnitude-10 probe report as mentioned previously.
\* The initial turnsheet will be enclosed with the initial printout and should be sent back to us before the date indicated on the printout.
\* All factory groups start with work in process already running.

## 3.6 RACE NUMBERS

Players are assigned race numbers as follows: Players 1 thru 15 to race 0, 16-30 to race 1, 31-45 to race 2, 46-60 to race 3, 61-75 to race 4, 76-90 to race 5, 91-105 to race 6, 106 to 120 to race 7, 121-135 to race 8, and 136-150 to race 9.
Games 1 and 2 of EMPYREAN CHALLENGE are the same, except that game 1 has 6 races of 25 each, and game 2 has 4 races of 25 each.

\* Indicates line has been revised or added on.
