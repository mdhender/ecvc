# 7. SHIPS AND COLONIES

## 7.1 TYPES

There are: ships (SHP), enclosed surface colonies (ESC), open surface colonies (OPC), and orbiting colonies (OBC).

## 7.2 LOCATION RESTRICTIONS

### 7.2.1 OPEN COLONIES

OPC's are not allowed on planets with a habitability number of 0.

### 7.2.2 ENCLOSED SURFACE COLONIES

ESC's may be on any planet.

### 7.2.3 ORBITING COLONIES

OBC's may be located in any orbit around a star, whether or not there is a planet there.

### 7.2.4 SHIPS

SHP's may be located in any orbit around a star, whether or not there is a planet there, and may be moved from orbit to orbit, or from its orbit to orbit 0 of another system.
All incoming ships arrive in orbit 0 of the system.

## 7.3 STRUCTURAL REQUIREMENTS

### 7.3.1 DEFINITION

Assembled STUN, LTSU, or SLSU must encompass the VOLUME of any s/c's units and population, except NMTS and METS in OPC's and ESC's.
The total volume units times the STRUCTURE RATIO, (see 7.3.1.1), gives the s/c structural requirement.
In OPC's and ESC's, NMTS and METS are stored outside the colony.

#### 7.3.1.1 STRUCTURE RATIO

The minimum STUN, LTSU, or SLSU required to enclose a single volume unit are:

|                                 |    |
|---------------------------------|----|
| Open colonies (OPC)             | 1  |
| Enclosed surface colonies (ESC) | 5  |
| Orbiting colonies (OBC)         | 8  |
| Ships (SHP)                     | 10 |

This means that structural requirements of a SHP are determined by how much it carries.
If a SHP is to transport 100,000 volume units, it will require a minimum of 1,000,000 STUN, LTSU, or SLSU.
For the number of volume units per item see the UNIT CHARACTERISTICS SUMMARY on the back cover.

### 7.3.2 STRUCTURAL REQUIREMENT EXCEEDED

Open colonies may exceed their structural requirement.
When this happens, POP are forced into the fields outside the colony to live.
Twenty-five percent of the dispossessed POP's will turn rebel each turn.
In ships, orbiting colonies, and enclosed surface colonies, structural requirement excesses prevent new items from entering the colony.
In the case of an excess of POP, LFS would not be sufficient, and some would die.
