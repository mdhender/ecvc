## 11.3 REBEL PICK-UPS AND TRANSFERS

Pickups of POP *never* include rebels.
If there are not enough loyal POP to make up the quantity specified in the order, then the amount picked up is reduced.
TFR's of POP *always* include rebels, unless the X option is used.
See Chapter 18, Rebellion, for further details regarding rebels.

### 11.3.1 X OPTION

If the X option is used, only loyal POP will transfer.
The X option is used in TFR's only, *never in any other order*.
In order to TFR loyal POP only, change the last letter of the POP to X.
For example, PRO would be PRX, SLD would be SLX, CNW would be CNX, etc.

## 11.4 SHORTAGES

Shortages of fuel or PRO's will result in all or part of the TFR or PUP orders failing.
TFR's and PUP's are done in the order in which they are listed on the turnsheet; they are done one by one until stopped by a shortage.

    NOTE: NEVER WRITE MORE THAN ONE TFR OR PUP ORDER FOR THE SAME ITEM TO THE SAME S/C ON THE SAME TURN, ie: 30, TFR, 40,000, METS, 26. and 30, TFR, 80,000, METS, 26.

This is very important as doing this can not only cause the order to abort, but can cause other errors in the turn, as well.
The order will also abort if there is not enough space in the receiving s/c to house the new items.

## 11.5 RSCH

RSCH are transferred, but are never picked up.
REC orders are unnecessary for them.
They do not use up any TPT capacity.
All other rules concerning TFR's apply to them.

## 11.6 JETTISONS

When a unit is jettisoned, it is dumped into space.
This is done by transferring the unit to s/c "0".

    HOME COLONIES MAY NEVER JETTISON THEIR POP.

Any type of POP may otherwise be jettisoned.
If POP's are jettisoned, then the rebels in that s/c will increase by 10 times the amount of POP jettisoned.
For example assume that 500 SLD are jettisoned, and the colony had 1,000 rebels.
10 times 500 (SLD) is 5,000.
5,000 is added to 1,000 rebels, making a total of 6,000 rebels in that colony.
