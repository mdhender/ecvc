# 18. REBELLION

Many of the rules listed below are approximations, meant to serve as a guide.
It is hard for a leader to know what is in the hearts of his subjects, so there is an element of unpredictability regarding rebels.
Rebels are the units in each population group (except spies and soldiers) who are willing to rebel.
Rebel soldiers are soldier units who are willing to rebel.
Spies do not rebel.

## 18.1 INCREASES

### 18.1.1 REBEL INCREASE CHART

| CAUSE OF INCREASE | APPROXIMATE AMOUNT OF INCREASE |
|---|---|
| Capture by a foreign power | 65% of loyal POP's become rebels. |
| \* Standard of living: .5 to 1 \*\* | 1 to 5% of current rebel pop. |
| \* Standard of living: .1 to .5 \*\* | 10-50% of current rebel pop. |
| \* Standard of living: below .1 \*\* | 50% or more of current rebel pop. |
| \* Food rationing | 1-75% of current rebel pop. |
| \* Starvation \*\* | 75% or more of current rebel pop. |
| Lack of housing (in OPC's only) | 25% of unhoused pop become rebels. |
| Jettisoning POP's. | 10 x number jettisoned become rebels. |
| POP's become under the control of a player of a different race. | 1% to 10% of those POP's become rebels |
| Back pay | The ratio of back pay to POP's determines the rate of increase. |
| Paying one POP group much lower or higher than other groups | 0 to 1% of current total population. |

### 18.1.2 REBEL SOLDIER INCREASES

Rebel soldiers increase due to disproportionate pay, at a rate of usually no more than 2% of the current total population.
Rebel soldiers increase in proportion to the number of rebels each turn.
The larger the quantity of rebels, the more rebel soldiers will be "recruited" each turn from the loyal SLD units.

## 18.2 DECREASES

If a s/c is captured by a foreign power, 65% of the rebels in that s/c become loyal.
Rebels and rebel soldiers also decrease when the standard of living rises, and when they are converted by group B SPYS back into loyal POP units.
A s/c's group B spies converts 1.5 rebels or rebel soldiers per turn.
Visiting group B spies convert only one rebel or rebel soldier per turn.
The group B spies convert the rebel soldiers first, and when done, convert rebels.

## 18.3 REBELLION

10% of all rebels are considered activists and will fight if a rebellion takes place.
All rebel soldiers will fight if a rebellion takes place.
