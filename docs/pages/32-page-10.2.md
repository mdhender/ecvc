### 10.4.1 RESEARCH POINTS

One way to improve a s/c's TL is to build the required amount of research points necessary to raise it.
A build change - research - order (BCH, RSCH) will cause a FCT group to change from manufacturing an item to building RSCH.
A FCT producing RSCH will build .25 times its TL in RSCH per turn.
The FCT group will complete its work-in-process before beginning the research.
RSCH may be transferred or sold, decreasing the total number of RSCH accumulated.
Once the required number of RSCH have been built, the computer automatically raises the TL of the colony, and decreases the number of RSCH accumulated.
The quantities of RSCH listed below for each level are the number required to get from the preceeding level to the one listed:

| LEVEL | RSCH       |
|-------|------------|
| 2     |    100,000 |
| 3     |    200,000 |
| 4     |    400,000 |
| 5     |    800,000 |
| 6     |  1,600,000 |
| 7     |  3,200,000 |
| 8     |  6,400,000 |
| 9     | 12,800,000 |
| 10    | 25,600,000 |
    
A REC order is not written by the recipient of a RSCH transfer.
Note that METS and NMTS are not required for the production of RSCH.

### 10.4.2 TRANSFERRING RSCH

The second way to improve a s/c's TL is to transfer RSCH from another s/c.
A s/c transferring RSCH will have that number deducted from the total RSCH accumulated.

When a s/c's established, it automatically starts out with TL-1, whether the establishing s/c has a higher TL or not.

TFR'ing RSCH requires TPT's, but RSCH do not use up any TPT capacity (see 11.1).

### 10.4.3 BUYING AND SELLING RSCH

The third way to improve a s/c's TL is to buy technology (RSCH) on the market.
A s/c selling RSCH will have that number deducted from the total RSCH accumulated.
