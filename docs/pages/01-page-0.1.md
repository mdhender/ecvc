# FOREWORD

Thank you for buying this rule book!
We hope that you decide to play our game after reading the rules.
The rules should be read twice: once quickly to absorb the general feel of the game; and again in order to gain an in-depth understanding of them.

It should be pointed out that nearly all the "bookkeeping" in the game is done by the computer, so that the games are easier to play than appears at first glance.

A free newsletter, "Odysseys", will be published quarterly, (more or less), which will: announce game winners, rate performances of the other players, announce new games and products, discuss strategy and tactics, and answer players' questions.
Your articles or letters will be welcome.

We wish to acknowledge the help of Rodney Rogers, J. M. Finley, Rick Goodman, Brian Sullivan, Jack Everitt, Ron Temple, and Wayne Lemmon for their work in revising this book; and we would like to thank Bill Ameling, Glenn Mouton, and *many* others for their ideas and suggestions.

Finally, we wish to thank Brett Tondreau for introducing computer gaming to us, and for all of his advice and encouragement.
Also, our thanks to The Flying Buffalo, Inc. for pioneering the field of computer gaming.
And, last but not least, we thank Metagaming for developing the science fiction simulation war game.

We hope that you find playing our games to be a rewarding and enjoyable gaming experience.
