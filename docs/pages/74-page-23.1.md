# APPENDIX I: MAXI-CHALLENGE

MAXI-CHALLENGE differs from EMPYREAN CHALLENGE in the following ways:

1. Each Ruler starts with the resources of an entire planet.
2. The News Service does not exist.
3. There are 20 players per game, instead of 150.
4. The market does not exist.
5. Gold does not exist in MAXI-CHALLENGE, as there will probably be no trade between players.
6. The efficiency factor used here is always **1** when calculating cost, and is never less than **1** when calculating personnel requirements.
   (See 8.3, Manufacturing).
7. The victory conditions are:
   - A. If all the existing colonies (not ships) belong to one Ruler and his Regents, they win and the game is over.
   - B. A Ruler and his Regents can win by scoring 15,000 victory points.
     No Victory points can be scored on turn 0.
     Fractions of points are dropped.
     Losing control of a s/c results in a loss of 20 victory points.
     The victory points consist of colony points, ship points, and planet points.
     - a. Colony points are counted as follows:
       - i. (Colony POP x standard of living) divided by 10,000,000.
         If the standard of living is higher than 2, the computer uses 2 for the standard of living figure.
       - ii. (FCT's that are assembled and not building RSCH or retooling x the FCT's TL) ÷ 1,000,000.
     - b. Ship points are counted as follows:
       - i. (Assembled HDRV's x each HDRV's TL) divided by 10,000 *OR* (SHP's mass divided by 0.6) divided by 10,000,000; WHICHEVER IS LESS.
     - c. Planet points are counted as follows:
       - i. Habitability number times 3.
       - ii. Habitability number plus 2 for M.C. fast games #1 and #2, slow game #1, and Designers Challenge.

The units a Ruler starts the game with are listed on the pre-game turnsheet.

Home planets are treated just as any other for purposes of control.
