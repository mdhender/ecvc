## 12.2 MARKET FOR GAMES 1, 2, AND 3 ONLY OF EMPYREAN CHALLENGE

### 12.2.1 WHO GETS THE MARKET REPORT

In these games, markets are only located on *home* planets; only members of that race will get a market report.

### 12.2.2 HOW THE MARKET WORKS

In these games, there is no market "stockpile".
The market does not buy and sell items, but coordinates transactions between players.
Any unit may be sold or bought, including population units, except for SPYS.
Only NON and UNA can be sold; assembled items must be DAS'd first before they can be sold.
S/c's are never sold.
All payment is made in gold units.
Players offer to buy things on the market with buy (BUY) orders, bidding for a quantity of an item at so many gold units apiece.
Players offer to sell things on the market with sell (SEL) orders, offering a quantity of an item at so many gold units apiece.
The market processes buy and sell orders one item at a time.
The highest buy order is compared to the lowest sell order.
If the buyer's price exceeds the seller's price by at least 1%, the transaction takes place.
If a transaction is made, and there are buy and sell orders remaining on that item, the process continues until no more transactions can be made.
If a transaction takes place using all of a buy order and part of a sell order, then the next lowest buy price is compared to the remainder of the sell order.
If a transaction takes place using all of a sell order and part of a buy order , then the next highest sell order is compared to the remainder of the buy order.
The transactions are tallied and prices for each item are averaged.
On the printout, under "Transactions", a list of items sold is shown, the total quantity per item, and average price per item.
Under "Bids", (offers to buy which failed), a list of failed BUY order items, their total quantity per item, and their average price per item.
Under "Offers", (sell offers which failed), a list of failed SEL order items, total quantity per item, and their average price per item.
The market transfers automatically the gold units and the units sold between the s/c's when a transaction has been made.

#### 12.2.2.1 Price the Buyer Pays and Seller Receives

The price per unit the buy will pay is determined by the following formula: ((B-S) / 2) + S = P.
B is the buyer's price, S is the seller's price, and P is the price the buyer pays.
P times .99 = the price the seller receives.

#### 12.2.2.2 Commissions

Commissions are paid by the buyer and go to the market, which is not under the control of any player.
The minimum commission received by the market on any transaction is one gold unit.
The minimum net payment (payment less commission) to any seller on any transaction is one gold unit.
Thus the cost to the buyer on any transaction is at least a total of 2 gold units.

### 12.2.3 BARTER

Players may barter if they wish.
There are no rules on bartering; but any disputes between the players in a barter must be settled between themselves.
They must arrange transportation for the units bartered themselves.
